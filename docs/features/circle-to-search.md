# Circle to Search

Circle anything on your screen, then choose what to do with it.

## How to

1. Start OpenCircle and press `Ctrl+Shift+Space`.
2. Draw a circle or drag a box around a picture or some text. Release to finish the selection.
3. Choose **Search in browser**, **Copy text**, or **Translate** in the bar.
4. For text, tap to place the selection start, double-click a word, or triple-click a line. Drag to select characters.
5. Press `Esc` to close the overlay.

## Shortcuts

| Shortcut | What it does |
|---|---|
| `Ctrl+Shift+Space` | Open Circle to Search |
| Double-click / triple-click text | Select a word / a line |
| `Ctrl+C` | Copy a box as an image, or selected words as text |
| `Esc` | Close the overlay |

## Tips

- **Search in browser** sends a picture selection to Google Lens in your own browser. A text selection opens a Google text search instead.
- OpenCircle uploads your picture nowhere itself. Your browser sends it directly to Lens; no image-hosting service sits in between.
- The default is to wait for your choice after you circle. You can change shortcuts and the automatic action in Settings.
- Unfinished features are in **Settings › Labs**.

## Limits

- Lens and Google text search need an internet connection and follow Google's policies.
- A selection that is one plain colour is not sent to Lens. Choose an area with a picture or text.
- If the question field has focus, the first `Esc` leaves the field; press it again to close.
